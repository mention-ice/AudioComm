Acoustic modem

Binary FSK over the air: one device transmits through its speaker, any device in the room decodes through its microphone.

What is being sent

Frame: 0.1 s warm-up · 127-bit MLS preamble · 24-bit header (type and size, each bit sent 3 times) · scrambled message · 127-bit MLS postamble. Bit 0 is a burst of the low tone, bit 1 a burst of the high tone. The same frame is produced by audioComm_tx.m and audiocomm.py.
